Question 4 • Displacement Analysis

A runner goes 100m East, then 100m West. What is their total displacement?

cancel
Your Answer 200m

0m

Correct Answer: 0m
Why this happened?

You likely calculated total distance instead of displacement. Displacement is a vector quantity that measures the change in position from start to finish. Since the runner ended where they started, displacement is zero.
